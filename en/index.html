Brand Story

That diagonal line —
where patience meets breakthrough.

The green-and-gold diagonal on every bottle isn't just decoration. Green is the quiet patience of slowly cold-brewing tea leaves; gold is the sweetness fruit gathers under the sun. Where the two colors meet is the exact moment "stillness" meets "breakthrough": a quiet wait, and then — in one instant — fruit aroma breaks through the tea, bright and unmistakable.

That's also what "忍" (Ren) means to us — not suppression, but a ninja's quiet precision: knowing exactly when to wait, and when to act. YI-REN has already worked out both.
